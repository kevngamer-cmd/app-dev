About the Series;

**Suits** is an American legal drama television series created by **Aaron Korsh**. It follows the lives of talented lawyers working at a law firm in New York City. The story mainly focuses on Harvey Specter, a successful lawyer, and Mike Ross, a young man with an amazing memory who works as a lawyer despite not having a law degree.

> "When you're backed against the wall, break the goddamn thing down."

- Strong friendship and teamwork.
- Unexpected plot twists.
- Harvey Specter's confidence and negotiation skills.
